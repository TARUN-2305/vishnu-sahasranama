# ಶ್ರೀ ವಿಷ್ಣು ಸಹಸ್ರನಾಮ · Śrī Viṣṇu Sahasranāma

The complete Śrī Viṣṇu Sahasranāma in Kannada script with IAST transliteration, for daily pārāyaṇa and verse-by-verse study (English meanings and Kannada word meanings). It installs as an app and works offline.

**Open it:** https://tarun-2305.github.io/vishnu-sahasranama/

On Android, open the link in Chrome, then menu ⋮ → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**). On iPhone, open it in Safari, then Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

## Sources

The Sanskrit text is from Sanskrit Wikisource, “श्रीविष्णुसहस्रनामस्तोत्रम्” (a conversion of the sanskritdocuments.org edition), available under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) licence, and was cross-checked against the Mahābhārata, Anuśāsana Parva (Kumbhakonam edition, Wikisource). The Kannada-script and IAST lines were transliterated from it by script; the Sanskrit text as presented here is shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. Editorial changes and textual variants are listed in the site's *Sources & textual notes*.

The English and Kannada meanings are this edition's own wording and have not yet been reviewed by a Sanskrit scholar.
